Frequently Asked Questions

How long does training take?

It depends on your current skill level and the package you choose. Generally, a complete course takes around 1 to 2 months.

What vehicles can I learn?

We provide training for Motorcycles, Three Wheelers, Cars (Manual & Auto), and Heavy Vehicles (Lorry, Bus, Prime Mover).

Do you help with learner permits?

Yes, we guide you through the entire process, including the RMV medical tests and documentation for the learner permit.

Can beginners join?

Absolutely! We specialize in training beginners from scratch in our dedicated safe training ground before moving to real roads.

Do you provide heavy vehicle training?

Yes, we are fully equipped and certified to provide training for Lorries, Buses, and Prime Movers.
