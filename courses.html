THE PROCESS

How to Get Your License

The official step-by-step roadmap to obtaining your driving license in Sri Lanka.

01

Medical Fitness Certificate

Visit the National Transport Medical Institute (NTMI) to undergo a basic physical and vision test to ensure you are medically fit to drive.

02

Written Exam & Learner's Permit

Register at the Department of Motor Traffic (RMV) and pass the written examination covering road signs and traffic rules to obtain your Learner's Permit.

03

Practical Training Period

Undergo rigorous practical training with our instructors. By law, you must hold your learner's permit for a minimum of 3 months before taking the practical test.

04

Practical Driving Test

Successfully complete the RMV practical driving examination with absolute confidence to receive your official smart-card driving license.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Age for Written Exam: Must be at least 17 years old to register and sit for the written test.
  • Age for Practical Exam: Must be at least 18 years old to sit for the practical test and receive the final license.
  • Identification: Must possess an original National Identity Card (NIC) or a valid passport with the NIC number.
  • Additional Documents: Original Birth Certificate (for first-time applicants) and the NTMI Medical Certificate.
INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the training take?

The duration depends entirely on your current skill level and availability. Generally, a complete course from absolute beginner to licensed driver takes approximately 1 to 2 months.

Do you assist with the learner's permit?

Yes. We provide end-to-end guidance, helping you schedule your NTMI medical exam and processing all paperwork required for the learner's permit.

Are beginners welcome?

Absolutely. We specialize in beginner instruction. Your initial practical training will take place in our exclusive private training grounds before you enter public traffic.
