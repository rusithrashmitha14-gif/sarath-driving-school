OUR LEADERSHIP

Generations of Trust

Nimal Amarasinghe

Founder & Visionary

Establishing Sarath Driving School in 1980, Sarath set an uncompromising standard for road safety and disciplined instruction. His pioneering methodologies laid the foundation for what would become the region's most trusted driver education institution.

Sujeewa Amarasinghe

Current Owner & Director

Continuing his father's legacy, Sujeewa has modernized the academy by integrating advanced dual-controlled fleets and updated theoretical curricula, while fiercely maintaining the foundational principles of safety and excellence his father instilled.

Our Vision

To be the absolute benchmark of driver education in Sri Lanka, producing a new generation of disciplined, highly-skilled drivers who define road safety and elevate driving standards nationwide.

Our Mission

To deliver elite, uncompromising instruction through a perfect synergy of traditional discipline and modern automotive technology, ensuring every student who graduates from our academy does so with absolute confidence and mastery of the road.

THE PROCESS

Your Journey to Mastery

01

Registration & Documentation

Begin your journey at our Veyangoda Head Office. We handle the paperwork, guiding you through the NTMI Medical Certificate process and Learner's Permit acquisition.

02

Theoretical Foundation

Engage in comprehensive classroom sessions covering advanced road rules, defensive driving strategies, and essential vehicle mechanics in our modern lecture halls.

03

Practical Excellence

Transition to our private training grounds for initial vehicle control, followed by rigorous on-road training with our elite, government-certified instructors.

04

The Final Examination

Execute the Department of Motor Traffic (RMV) practical test with absolute confidence, precision, and mastery to earn your license.
